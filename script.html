How It Works — 3 Phases
Phase 1
Fridge Vision + Smart Recipes
Snap a photo of your fridge. Gemini identifies every ingredient and flags what's expiring. We suggest 3 recipes and surface exactly what's missing.
Gemini 2.0 Flash · Backboard Memory
Phase 2
Pantry Locator Agent
Based on your missing ingredients, we find nearby food pantries using Google Places and rank them by distance, open hours, and past availability.
Google Places API · MongoDB Geospatial · Gemini Ranker
Phase 3
Voice Swarm + Live Results
An AI agent calls up to 5 pantries simultaneously, asks about availability and hours, and streams live transcripts to your dashboard and a Google Sheet.
ElevenLabs Conversational AI · Twilio · SSE Live Stream
→ Results feed into a Mapbox-optimized pickup route — one tap to open in Apple or Google Maps.
